WakeCraft Manager — Terms of Service

Effective date: November 13, 2026

WakeCraft Manager is a private, noncommercial administration utility operated for the World of WakeCraft Discord guild server. It is not a general-purpose public bot or a service with guaranteed availability.

Authorized use

Only the server owner or a designated administrator should authorize and run the utility. Use must comply with Discord's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, developer rules, and applicable laws.

What it may change

When explicitly run with its apply option, the utility may create or organize Discord categories, text channels, voice channels and roles; update selected channel permission settings; publish or update guild-information messages; and schedule Discord events. Administrators should review the read-only audit and proposed changes before applying them. Certain permission changes can affect members' access to channels.

Community conduct and trade

All members must follow the World of WakeCraft guild rules. Harassment, scams, sharing account passwords, real-money trading through guild channels, and attempts to compromise accounts or Discord permissions are not permitted. The guild is responsible for its own member conduct, gameplay decisions, and moderation.

No account access or payments

The utility does not ask guild members to provide Blizzard or Discord passwords, process payments, or provide items or in-game services for sale. Never send personal credentials or a bot token to another guild member.

Availability and changes

The utility is provided as-is for the guild administrator's use. Configuration changes may be reviewed or reversed by guild administrators. The operator may stop using the tool or update these terms if its functionality changes.

Questions

Contact the World of WakeCraft server owner or an officer through the guild server regarding configuration, access, or these terms.
